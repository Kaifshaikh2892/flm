# Upstream sources

- **LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct**, Liquid AI: [model and license](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct), revision `0f604ada3f766f9f257460c4c9f0b5d6f69d431b`. The LFM Open License v1.0 applies to the upstream weights; those weights are not licensed under this repository's MIT license. Review its conditions before redistribution or commercial use. The downloader retains the upstream model card and license.
- **SmolLM2-135M-Instruct**, HuggingFaceTB: [optional reference model](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct), revision `12fd25f77366fa6b3b4b768ec3050bf629380bac`. Apache-2.0. This is a legacy reference, not the default conversational backbone.
- **Everyday Conversations / SmolTalk**, HuggingFaceTB: [source dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceTB/everyday-conversations-llama3.1-2k) and [collection](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceTB/smoltalk), revision `5feaf2fd3ffca7c237fc38d1861bc30365d48ffa`. The source card declares Apache-2.0 and describes synthetic-data provenance. Downloaded corpus files are not bundled.
- **MaleCNS v1.0**, FlyEM (HHMI Janelia), University of Cambridge Department of Zoology, MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, and Google Research: [official source and attribution](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/), CC BY 4.0. Both original files are pinned by SHA-256. The preparation script retains annotated non-glial nodes, filters both endpoints, and normalizes incoming contact counts. Derived graph data remain in the local cache. Official public data download URLs are not deployment infrastructure for this project.
- **PyTorch, Transformers, NumPy, SciPy, PyArrow, Safetensors, Hugging Face Hub**, and their dependencies retain their upstream licenses.

No upstream model weights, corpus files, or connectome arrays are committed. The original synthetic examples in `training/conversation-style.json` are covered by this project's MIT license. No code from nanoGPT, nanochat, or MLX-LM is vendored.
